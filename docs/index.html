A short primer

How Enigma transforms a letter

  1. The right rotor steps before every keypress.
  2. Current travels right-to-left through all three rotors.
  3. The reflector sends it back through the inverse wirings.
  4. A different lamp illuminates, and the circuit opens on release.

When a rotor reaches its notch, it turns its neighbor. The middle rotor can move on two consecutive presses—the famous double-step.
